## Structure
The pantheon consists of four levels of hierarchy.
1. The Elders: Four Beings that act as the anchor of existence. Without them, the world itself would unravel.
2. Council: Several major deities whose domains are far-reaching and exist across many cultures. Their appearances, personalities, and powers often differ depending on which culture they appear to. In a room where each person is from a different country, it is likely they would see different versions of the same deity, should they appear in the room with them.
3. Sovereign: Thousands of minor deities that often have limited strength. Perhaps they rule a niche domain (like knitting or pets), or only control a single landmass such as a river or mountain. Some Sovereign are considered pieces of a Councilmember: still their own person, but part of a larger whole.
4. Agents: Servants to the three above. Their powers are derived from their lords, and are considered godlike in their own right. The line between an Agent and their mortal counterparts, such as Warlocks, Paladins, and Clerics, is rather muddied. However, a rule of thumb is that Agents often live in the realm of the spiritual and cannot die by normal means.
